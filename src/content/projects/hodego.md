## What it was

Hodego connected athletes with world-class coaches and sports experts for personalized training, remote guidance, and in-competition advice from people who've competed at the highest levels of the sport.

## Why it mattered

The craft of elite coaching has always been locked behind proximity — you had to know the right person, live in the right city, afford the right club. Hodego was built to break that constraint open with remote access, on-demand sessions, and live competition support, for an enormous market of serious recreational athletes who never had a way in.

## My role

As co-founder, I led across product definition and technical delivery rather than serving as the primary React engineer. I translated the concept into features, evaluated architecture and vendor choices, coordinated a three-developer team in India, maintained my own Git branch, and tested the application daily.

The work included decisions around Stripe payments, Agora video, profiles, booking, calendar behavior, and scheduling. I reproduced bugs, prioritized fixes, reviewed whether delivered behavior matched the requirement, and proposed changes through Git.

That distinction matters: the case study is evidence of technical product leadership, architecture judgment, and hands-on delivery participation—not a claim that I personally authored the entire application.

## How it ended

The MVP ran in production: payments, live video, booking, scheduling. It went offline when we migrated hosting platforms without verified, restorable backups and lost the production database. The marketplace has not come back, and this chapter is effectively closed.

That failure is on the record here deliberately. Every controls habit in this casebook — explicit blast radius before irreversible operations, backups proven restorable before a migration, rollback paths treated as requirements instead of afterthoughts — is priced against exactly this outcome. Hodego is where I stopped treating those controls as optional.
